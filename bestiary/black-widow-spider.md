**Does not match either MM spider used elsewhere in this bestiary.** It is
closer in flavour to the MM's **GIANT SPIDER** (a web-builder that traps prey
and drops on it, MM p.90) than to the **HUGE SPIDER** used for B1's *crab
spiders* (a roving hunter with no fixed web, area 52) — but its own hit dice
(3, not the giant spider's 4+4) and damage (2-12, not 2-8) don't match either
MM entry's numbers. **B1's own line is used for combat outright**; only the
poison's *consequence* needed an outside source, since B1 doesn't say what a
failed save does.

## Poison — DM ruling, day 139

B1 says only "+poison." The MM's giant spider entry gives the operative rule,
borrowed here as the closest thematic match (web-builder, ambush-from-web):
*"The bite of a giant spider is poisonous. A victim must save versus poison or
be killed."* No stated bonus or penalty to the save either way.

*source: **Monster Manual p. 90**, SPIDER, "Giant Spider" paragraph, OCR text

## Play record

**Day 139, expedition 30, the mouth of area 42.** Encountered after killing
both of area 41's troglodytes; alerted early by a failed Move Silently check
on a solo scout, then held still rather than reveal itself. Surprise rolled
(party 5, spider 4) and, after a mid-resolution correction (the DM had wrongly
applied the HUGE spider's "surprises prey on 1-5" trait, which belongs to a
different creature and a different room's monster), resolved under the
standard 1-2 threshold: **neither side surprised**. Frython's *sleep* put it
under (1d4 roll for the 2+1-to-3 HD band, the only creature present); the
party then killed it under the same day's ruling that *sleep*'s "automatic
slaying" is not melee-only (PHB p.68 does not say so) — an attack made with
intent to kill, missile or melee, slays a sleeper outright once it is
declared. XP 60 + 4×13 = 112, all ten shares.
