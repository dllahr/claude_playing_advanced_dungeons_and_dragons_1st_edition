# Fire Beetle

**Met day 99 in AREA 12, THE LIBRARY** — caged, alive, and unreachable. Never
fought. Recorded because the party examined them closely and asked the right
question about them, and because **their glow-glands are the standing answer to
this campaign's oldest problem, which is light.**

## The glands — why anybody cares

> *"Fire beetles have **two glands above their eyes and one near the back of
> their abdomen** which give off a red glow. For this reason they are highly
> prized by miners and adventurers, as **this luminosity will persist for from
> 1-6 days after the glands are removed from the beetle. The light shed
> illuminates a 10' radius**."* — MM p. 10

| | |
|---|---|
| glands per beetle | **3** — two above the eyes, one at the back of the abdomen |
| radius | **10 feet** |
| duration after removal | **1-6 days, rolled per gland** |
| fuel, refills, wicks, hooding | **none. It is not a flame.** |

**Three glands off one beetle is potentially three light sources for up to six
days each, needing nothing.** A lantern is 30 ft for 4 turns a flask; a gland is
10 ft for *days*, and cannot be blown out, does not need hooding before a rest,
and cannot set anything alight. It is worse light and incomparably better
logistics.

**They are also loot with a shelf life.** A gland found in a dead beetle is worth
nothing a week later, so the roll happens when it is taken, not when it is used.

## Behaviour

- *"Found both above and below ground, being **primarily nocturnal**."*
- **Non-intelligent.** No parley, no morale bargain, no reaction roll beyond the
  table. It bites what disturbs it.
- 3-12 appearing. Nothing in the entry says they are territorial or lair-bound —
  `% IN LAIR: Nil` and `TREASURE TYPE: Nil`.

## Special attacks

None. The entry prints *Nil*. **The glow is not an attack and does not blind.**

## Special defences

None printed. They are not fire-resistant despite the name — nothing in the
entry grants it, and *"fire"* describes the glow, not the creature.

## The caged ones in area 12 — and the question the module refuses to answer

B1 p.12, verbatim:

> *"Small cages inset into the north wall contain numerous fire beetles, and
> these unusual insects give off an eerie, glowing light from their bodies —
> enough to illuminate this portion of the room… **The insects themelves seem to
> be thriving in their captive abode, but their food source and longevity are
> totally puzzling** . . . There is no way to open or force the cages themselves,
> so **releasing the insects or gaining access to them is impossible to any
> adventurers**."*

**Two rulings this forces, and both must be held:**

1. **The cages cannot be opened, forced, or reached into. Ever.** Not by tools,
   not by cleverness, not by a good idea at the table. The module says
   *impossible*, and the party is chronically short of light — which makes this
   exactly the prohibition that will be tested. **Hold it.**
2. **The food and the longevity have NO ANSWER.** The module flags it as
   *puzzling* and supplies nothing. So there is nothing to find, nothing to
   deduce, and **no hidden-outcome roll** — a search of the cages is a true
   negative and is reported as finding nothing. **Do not invent a solution**
   (a feed chute, a spell, an illusion, a space behind the wall). Inventing one
   would be authoring content the module deliberately left open, and it is more
   interesting unanswered.

**What honest close examination gives them**, all observable and all given
freely: they are unmistakably beetles; the glow comes from three discrete
points on each body and not from the whole of it; they move, so they are alive;
and **the cage floors are bare — no food, no droppings, no husks, no shells, no
hopper, no chute, nothing.**

**And the arithmetic the party can do for itself:** a removed gland glows for
**at most six days**. These have been glowing for thirty-odd years. So these are
not dead beetles with persistent glands — **they are alive now**, and something
has kept them that way. *(Let the player reach that. Do not walk them to it.)*

## Play record

**Day 99, expedition 13, round ~325 — area 12, the library.** Examined at close
range and prodded with a ten-foot pole through the grille, after the player
asked whether they were really beetles and whether anything had ever fed them.
**Never attacked, never reachable, and worth more as a question than as loot.**

**No gland has ever been taken in this campaign.** The first dead fire beetle
the party is standing over is 1-6 days of light per gland, and by then they will
have forgotten this entry exists. That is what it is for.
