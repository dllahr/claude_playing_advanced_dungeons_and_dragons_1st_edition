## Behaviour that matters at the table

> *"Giant rats will avoid attacking strong parties unless commanded to fight by
> such creatures as wererats or vampires. They are fearful of fire and flee from
> it."* — MM p. 81

**They are afraid of fire.** A torch or a flask of oil moves them without a
morale check being needed at all. And a group that presses an attack on a strong
party is a signal: something is commanding it.
