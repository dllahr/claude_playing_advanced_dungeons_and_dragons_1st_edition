A foot or more of segmented vermin. First met camped in Rogahn's Chamber,
expedition 4.

**XP value 30 + 1/hp** — *DMG Appendix E, p. 197* (see `rules/experience.md`;
the "+n/hp" is per ACTUAL HIT POINT, not per hit die).

**`Damage: Nil` is the whole creature.** A giant centipede takes no hit points
off anybody, ever. It bites, and the bite carries poison. A party can stand in a
room full of them losing nothing at all — and then one character fails one save.
**Never roll damage for a centipede bite.** There is none to roll.

**Move 15"** — faster than anything in the party. You cannot walk away from these.

**Armor Class 9 and a quarter of a hit die.** Anything that hits one kills it.

## Special attacks

| Attack | Trigger | Save | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Poison | any bite that lands | vs poison **at +4** | see the ruling below |

> *"They are aggressive and rush forth to bite their prey, injecting poison into
> the wound, but in many cases this poison is weak and not fatal (**add +4 to
> saving throw die roll**)."* — MM p. 14

**DM RULING 2026-08-28, and it is a ruling, not a rule.** 1e poison is normally
save-or-die, but the Monster Manual says this poison is *"weak and not fatal"* in
as many words, and giving it save-or-die would contradict the text that also
grants the +4. So: **a failed save is incapacitation, not death.** The victim is
violently ill — **no attacks, no spells, no strenuous action, half movement, for
1-4 turns.** Recorded here so it is the same every time it comes up.
