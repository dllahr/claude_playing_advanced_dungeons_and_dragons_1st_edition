## Special attacks

None listed, but note the fluid itself: *"The fluids excreted by these
creatures dissolve flesh (3-12 hit points per round of exposure)"* -- this
reads as ongoing contact damage, not just the single listed attack.

## Special defences

**Striking it with lightning divides it into one or more smaller creatures,
each doing half normal damage.** Cold and fire have normal effect. No mention
of edged-weapon splitting (that is the GELATINOUS CUBE/black pudding
confusion to avoid) -- **this entry has no such rule**, only the lightning
split. Do not invent one.

Can flow through small spaces, travels walls and ceilings with ease.
