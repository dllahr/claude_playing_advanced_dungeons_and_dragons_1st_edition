# Carrion Crawler

**The single most dangerous placed encounter on B1 level 1.** Eight attacks a
round, every one of them a save-or-be-paralysed, against a party of six with 2-9
hit points each. It does **no hit point damage whatsoever** — that is not mercy,
it is the entire problem.

## Special attacks

| Attack | Trigger | Save | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tentacles x8 | every round, each independently | **save vs paralyzation** or paralysed | no damage at all |

> *"The head is equipped with 8 tentacles which flail at prey; each 2' long
> tentacle exudes a gummy secretion which when fresh, will paralyze opponents
> (save versus paralyzation or it takes effect). As there are so many tentacles
> with which to hit, and thus multiple chances of being paralyzed, these
> monsters are greatly feared."* — MM p. 13

**Eight separate to-hit rolls per round, and every hit is a save.** Against six
characters that is a paralysed party in two or three rounds if it is not killed
fast or avoided entirely.

## Special defences — and the thing that will surprise the party

> *"A carrion crawler moves quite rapidly on its multiple legs despite its bulk,
> and **a wall or ceiling is as easily traveled as a floor**, for each of the
> beast's feet are equipped with sharp claws which hold it fast."* — MM p. 13

**It climbs walls and ceilings at its full 12" move.** Closing a door on it or
backing down a corridor does not work the way it would against a walking thing,
and it can be ABOVE the party. The chamber's ceiling height matters.

**AC 3 head, AC 7 body.** The head is what carries the tentacles. Attacking the
body is much easier and is the sane choice for a 1st-level fighter needing an 18
against AC 3 versus a 14 against AC 7.

## Play record

Sighted at round 1477, expedition 4, at (21,28) — the far north-west corner of
area 5 — from the threshold at (21,25), at exactly 30 feet, the limit of lantern
reach. Party spotted it on a deliberate careful look (d6=4, needed 1-4).
Killed at (18,27), clinging to the south wall beside a door it could not open.

**KILLED, round 1503, expedition 4.** hp were 15 (3d8 = 6+7+1, +1).

Two volleys and a pursuit. First volley at 30 ft took 13 of its 15 and it broke
(morale 93 vs 61, flee in panic) and outran the party -- 12" to their 9", and
their lantern reaches 30 ft. But it fled WEST into the far end of a 90-ft
dead-end room with two closed doors it could not open, and it went UP the wall,
which is what the Monster Manual says it does.

**The party found it because they declared "advance west scanning the ceiling and
walls" before knowing where it was** -- the exact counter to its special defence.
Second volley at 20 ft: 16 damage from four hits. Dead.

**The lesson for the bestiary: this creature's edge is that it does not have to be
on the floor. A party that only looks at floor level will walk under it.**
**XP if killed: 580 + 4x15 = 640.** Split six ways that is ~107 each before the
+10% prime-requisite bonus — MORE THAN THE PARTY HAS EARNED IN FOUR EXPEDITIONS
COMBINED. Dave is on 352 XP; this one creature would take him past 460.

That is the trade the module is offering: the biggest prize on the level, guarded
by the one thing that can paralyse the whole party in three rounds.

## What a careful examination of the corpse reveals

The party prodded it with a 10-ft pole from ten feet back before approaching, and
then examined it without touching. All of the following is legitimately visible
and is what the MM description supports:

- **The head is armoured and the body is not.** A hard chitinous plate over the
  front, and behind it nine feet of soft segmented grub-body. This is exactly the
  AC 3 / AC 7 split, and a party that sees it can choose its target next time.
- **The tentacles are wet.** Each of the eight carries a clear gummy coating that
  is still glistening on a dead creature. This is the paralytic. The MM says it
  works *"when fresh"*.
- **The feet are hooks.** Dozens of short legs ending in curved claws — the
  wall-and-ceiling movement is mechanical, not magical, and obvious once seen.
- **The mouth is wrong for killing.** It is a scavenger's feeding mouth, with no
  bite worth the name. Put together with the tentacles the conclusion is plain
  and the party can reach it: **it does not kill its prey, it paralyses it and
  eats it where it lies.**

**DM RULING — harvesting the secretion.** If the party asks, the answer is that
the coating is still fresh NOW and will not be for long. Ruled 2026-08-29: it
degrades within a turn or two of the creature's death and cannot be stored,
bottled or applied to weapons by a party with no alchemist and no proper
vessel. **Anyone handling it barehanded rolls a save vs paralyzation.** Do not
volunteer any of this; answer it if asked.

**Nothing noxious is released by prodding the body.** There is no death throe, no
burst, no spore. The pole was a sound precaution and cost them nothing.
