## Special attacks / defences

None listed (Nil/Nil). **Infravision 60', and fight well in full daylight too**
-- no sunlight combat penalty, unlike goblins/kobolds/orcs.

## Behaviour that matters at the table

Tribal, organized -- a leader (sergeant) + 2 assistants (9hp each) per 20
encountered, a subchief at 100+, chiefs/bodyguards in the lair. Bully
nearby orcs/goblins. Two tribes meeting each other bicker (85%) or fight
(15%) absent a strong leader present.
