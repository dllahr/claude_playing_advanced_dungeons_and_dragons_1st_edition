*source: **Monster Manual p. 57**, 200 dpi page render 2026-08-26.*

**XP value 5 + 1/hp** — *DMG Appendix E, p. 205* (see `rules/experience.md`;
the "+n/hp" is per ACTUAL HIT POINT, not per hit die).

**Move 6" — they are slow.** A party at 9" outruns them, and a party at 12"
leaves them behind entirely. This is the one thing about kobolds worth
remembering tactically.

## Weapons carried

Spiked wooden club 30%, axe 20%, javelins (2-3) 15%, short sword and spear 10%,
short sword 10%, spear 10%, short sword and javelin 5%. Shields are wood or
wickerwork.

## Leaders

For every 40 kobolds there is a leader and two guards **equal to goblins** —
4 hit points, armor class 6, damage 1-6.
