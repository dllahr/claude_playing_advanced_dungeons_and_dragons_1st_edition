# Berserker

Human. MM lists them under **MEN**, not as a monster in their own right — which
is why the module's block leaves out everything that matters about them.

## Berserker stat block

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Armor Class | 7 |
| Hit Dice | 2-7 hit points |
| Move | 12" |
| Number of Attacks | 1 (or 2) |
| Damage | by weapon type |
| Save As | F1 |
| Treasure Type | K individually; B in lair |
| Intelligence | average to very |
| Alignment | Neutral |
| Size | M |
| Number Appearing | 10-100 |
| Surprised On | 2 |
| Surprises On | 2 |
| Morale | never checks |

*source: **Monster Manual p. 67**, MEN (Berserker), 200 dpi page render 2026-08-26.*

**Hit dice are hit POINTS, not dice** — 2-7 of them, so a berserker has fewer
hit points than a 1 HD monster and dies to a single good blow. One was described
shrugging off 7 damage on 5 hit points; he was already dead. **Check the number
before narrating a berserker surviving anything.**

For the DMG p. 75 attack matrix this campaign has used the **`1`** column. The
strictly literal reading of "2-7 hit points" is the `up to 1-1` column, one step
worse — flagged here, not silently changed, because three encounters were run
the other way.

## Special attacks

| Attack | Trigger | Save | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Battle lust | always, in melee | none | **strike twice per round, OR once at +2 to hit** — the attacker's choice |

> *"They scorn armor and engage in combat mad with battle lust. This lust
> enables them to strike twice, or once with a +2 to hit."* — MM p. 67

Neither half of that is in B1's stat block. Both halves have decided fights here.

## Special defences

None. AC 7 is no armour at all — they scorn it.

## Morale

> *"Berserkers never check morale. If they decide that a fight would bring them
> honor (and possibly loot) they will attack."* — MM p. 67

**Do not roll morale for berserkers.** They do not fall back, disengage, flee or
surrender. The only thing that has ever moved them off this party was Blartho's
*command* spell — they are men, they understand common, and *command* allows no
saving throw against anything under 6 hit dice.
