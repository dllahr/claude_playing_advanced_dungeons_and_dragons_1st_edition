## Behaviour that matters at the table

Tribal society, strongest rules the rest. Hate gnomes and dwarves, attack them
in preference to any other creature. Slave-takers, fond of torture. Dwell in
caves and dismal underground places by preference. For every 40 encountered,
a leader and 4 assistants fighting as orcs with 7 hp each.

## Weapons carried

Short sword & military pick 10%, short sword & sling 10%, short sword & spear
10%, sling 10%, morning star 20%, military pick 10%, spear 30%. Leaders and
guards carry two weapons each, the best available.

## Play record

**Day 140, round 152, level 2 near (12,29).** Wandering check, 7 goblins,
striking distance in a corridor. Tristle not surprised (d6=4); goblins
surprised (d6=2, 2 segments lost). His Move Silently (30%) failed (rolled 80),
so no extra circumstance pip toward his own surprise chance — made no
difference, since the roll already favoured him.

Short bow, 3x normal rate of fire during a surprise segment (DMG p.62) = 6
shots/segment x 2 segments = **12 arrows**, assigned 2 each to five goblins and
1 each to the remaining two. 5 hits of 12: one natural 20. Results — hp 5,5,4,4,3,2,1
assigned in order: goblin at 5hp untouched; goblin at 5hp hit once (1 dmg,
4hp left); goblin at 4hp hit twice (6 dmg) — **dead**; goblin at 4hp untouched;
goblin at 3hp hit once (2 dmg, nat 20, 1hp left); goblin at 2hp untouched;
goblin at 1hp hit once (5 dmg) — **dead**.

**2 killed, 5 remaining** (full/full/4hp/1hp/full across the survivors).
Morale check at +5% (25%+ casualties, DMG p.67): base 50, roll 66+5=71 vs 50 —
broke by 21%, **disengage and retreat**. Surviving five fell back down the
corridor rather than closing.
