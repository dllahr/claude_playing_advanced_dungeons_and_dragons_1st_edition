## Where a sage is, and who may hire one

> *"**Location Of A Sage:** Sages will be found only in large towns and cities.
> They are typically in or near colleges, schools, universities, libraries,
> museums, forums, and public speaking places. Sages belong to a Brotherhood,
> but as a general rule, this association is informal and not likely to have a
> headquarters at which a sage could be located."*

*source: DMG p. 32*

**Consulting is open to anybody. HIRING is not.** Only **fighters, paladins,
rangers, thieves and assassins** may hire a sage permanently, and a permanent
hire requires a **stronghold** with room for him. *(DMG p. 32.)* Asking a
question is *consulting*, and that is what this party wants.

## Short-term employment — what it actually costs

> *"Costs for short-term employment are **100 gold pieces per day** plus the
> variable amount shown under Information Discovery."*

Short-term employment **cannot last beyond one week**, and afterward the sage is
unavailable for at least a game month. *source: DMG p. 33*

### Information Discovery Time And Cost Table

| Question is | General | Specific | Exacting | G.P. Costs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Out Of Fields | 1–6 r. | 2–24 d. | — | 100/d. |
| In Minor Field | 1–4 r. | 2–20 d. | 5–40 d. | 1,000/d. |
| In Major Field | 1–3 r. | 1–12 d. | 3–30 d. | 500/d. |
| In Special Category | 1–2 r. | 1–10 h. | 2–12 d. | 200/d. |

*source: DMG p. 33*

`r.` = rounds · `h.` = hours · `d.` = days.

### Chance Of Knowing Answer To A Question

| Question is | General | Specific | Exacting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Out Of Fields | 31%–50% | 11%–20% | — |
| In Minor Field | 46%–65% | 31%–40% | 11%–20% |
| In Major Field | 61%–80% | 57%–60% | 26%–35% |
| In Special Category | 81%–100% | 76%–96% | 61%–80% |

*source: DMG p. 32*

**Roll d10 or d20 to fix the sage's base percentage within the range shown, then
roll d100 against it.**

> #### THE TWO COST WAIVERS, WHICH ARE EASY TO MISS
>
> - *"if the percentile dice score rolled for knowing the answer is in the
>   **lower 20% of the spread**, then there will be no costs incurred, as the
>   material is on hand"*
> - *"in the **special category** of study, **any spread within the lower 80%
>   has no cost**"*
>
> So a special-category question answered well is **100 gp/day and nothing
> else**. *source: DMG p. 33*

**Not knowing is expensive too:** *"Unknown information will always require from
51% to 100% of the maximum time shown to determine that the knowledge is beyond
the ability of the sage. All costs will accrue at only half of the stated
amount."* **And if the town is not nearby, DOUBLE the times and costs.**

## Fields of study

Six major fields, rolled or chosen: **Humankind 01-30 · Demi-Humankind 31-50 ·
Humanoids & Giantkind 51-60 · Physical Universe(s) 61-70 · Fauna 71-80 · Flora
81-90 · Supernatural & Unusual 91-00.**

**The one this campaign cares about is Supernatural & Unusual**, whose special
categories are: Astrology & Numerology · Cryptography · **Divination** ·
**Dweomercraeft** · Heraldry, Signs & Sigils · Medicine · Metaphysics · Planes
(Astral, Elemental & Ethereal) · Planes (Outer).

**A made magic item is `Dweomercraeft`.** *source: DMG p. 32*

> ### ⚠ THE WHOLE COST TURNS ON WHETHER IT IS HIS SPECIAL CATEGORY
>
> *"What is this ring?"* is a **specific** question. Against a sage whose special
> category is Dweomercraeft that is **1–10 hours, 76–96% to know, and usually no
> research fee at all** — call it a day and 100 gp. Against a sage for whom it is
> merely a **major field** it is **1–12 days at 500 gp/day and 57–60%**.
>
> **Same question, same ring: ~100 gp or ~3,000 gp.** So the first thing to
> establish about any sage is not whether he is learned but *what he is learned
> in*, and that is a question the party can ask for free.

## Rest and recuperation

After more than one day answering, a sage needs **1 day of rest for every 3 spent
in research**, and will not answer general queries during it. Pestering him
during that time costs **1–2 additional days of "special research"**, and until
it is granted *"the sage will expend the maximum amounts of time and expense in
answering questions."* *source: DMG p. 33*

## A sage is a person, not a lookup

> *"Any question asked must be within the scope of knowledge of the player
> character, or his or her associates at the time, and such inquiries must always
> be consistent with the learning of the milieu."* *(DMG p. 32.)*

And the DMG's own warning about the persona: *"it is therefore very important for
the DM to assume not only the role but also the overview and personal dedication
of the character."* A sage who is asked something outside his fields *"will hedge
and talk around the point, or just possibly sit and look wise for 4-6 rounds"* —
**and charge nothing, because no day was spent researching.**
