# B1 — the module's own standing rules

Everything here is stated in B1's front matter and applies to the whole dungeon
unless a room says otherwise. **Read this before the module's room key**, because
these override or precede the general PHB/DMG procedure.

*Found 2026-08-28, after twenty hours of play, because the door rule was
stumbled on while grepping for something else. The introduction had never been
read end to end. See `CHECKLIST.md` — read a module's front matter FIRST.*

## Doors

| d6, rolled ONCE per door | Result |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | **Locked** — bars entrance until picked or broken |
| 3-6 | **It opens** |

> *"In all cases, unless otherwise noted, doors will be locked one-third of the
> time — and any roll of a 1 or 2 on a six-sided die (d6) will mean that they
> will bar entrance unless the lock is sprung or broken. Breaking the lock or
> breaking down the entire door will be a noisy undertaking, to be sure, and may
> serve to attract unwelcome monsters."*

**No PHB open-doors roll for ordinary B1 doors** — that roll is for *stuck* doors
and B1 never calls its doors stuck. Breaking one is explicitly noisy: an extra
wandering check by the module's own words.

Doors are **heavy wood, five or six inches thick**.

## THE AIR RESISTS COMBUSTION

> *"The air within the caverns is heavy, wet, and musty. In some portions of the
> complex, a layer of dust lies upon everything, undisturbed for years. **Burning
> anything within is slow and difficult, for the entire atmosphere resists
> combustion. Torches and lanterns will burn smokily.**"*

**This has not been applied for the whole of expeditions 1-4** and the party is
carrying twenty-six flasks of oil as its main reserve weapon.

**DM rulings, 2026-08-28** — the module gives no numbers, so these are stated
once and stand:

| | Ruling |
|---|---|
| **A thrown oil flask** | still works. It carries its own fuel and its own flame. |
| **Fire spreading** | **it does not.** Nothing catches from something else burning. A fire that is not fed goes out. |
| **Setting something alight deliberately** | slow — a turn of work, not a round, and it needs its own fuel |
| **Torch and lantern burn times** | **unchanged.** The module says *smokily*, not *briefly*. |
| **Smoke** | real, and it accumulates. A hooded lantern in a **sealed, spiked room** with cloaks under the door will foul the air over a long stay. Worth remembering — the party has done this four times. |

## Construction

- Corridors are generally **10 feet wide**; ceilings **8 to 10 feet**
- The rock is **heavy blackish slate**, evident throughout both levels
- The upper level is finished stonework and masonry, in good repair

## The two levels

> *"The two levels of the dungeon are approximately equal in size and are located
> one above the other. If the two maps could be placed over one another, **the
> three access points between levels would directly correspond** to their
> locations on the maps and lead directly to each other up and down."*

**There are exactly THREE ways between the levels**, and they are vertically
aligned. When level 2 is extracted, its access points must land on the same
coordinates as level 1's.

## Wandering monsters

Check **every second turn, 1 in 6**; on a hit, d6 on the level's table, then
check surprise. *(The campaign uses the 20-round anchor plus hidden 1d6-1 jitter
described in `rules/exploration.md`, which averages to exactly this rate.)*

## The entrance

The outer door **"opens freely"** — no roll — and close examination shows chipped
wood at the edge where it was forced open long ago. Without deliberate
examination there is a **10% chance per adventurer, cumulative**, of noticing.

*source: **B1 module introduction**, "THE DUNGEON" and "KEY TO THE UPPER LEVEL",
read end to end 2026-08-28.*
