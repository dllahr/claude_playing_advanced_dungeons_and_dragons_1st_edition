| Burden | |
|---|---|

*source: **DMG p. 58**.*

## Terrain

| Terrain | |
|---|---|
| Normal | open ground, scrub, typical desert, light forest, low hills, small watercourses. **For vehicles it assumes ROADWAYS through such terrain, or smooth fields** |
| Rugged | rough ground, snow, forests, steep hills, large watercourses. **For vehicles, roadways through such terrain OR tracks/paths through normal terrain** |
| Very Rugged | broken ground, deep snow and ice, heavy forests, marshy ground, bogs, bluffs, mountains, broad watercourses |

*source: **DMG p. 58**, the definitions printed beside the two tables. Page
render 2026-09-12.*

## ⚠ WHAT THIS CAMPAIGN USES, AND THE FIGURE IT CORRECTS

**The hundred's roads are NORMAL terrain**, which for vehicles means *roadways
through such terrain* — that is what a road IS in the DMG's sense.

| | miles/day | |
|---|---|---|
| **cart** | **25** | Osk's run, Halm's carts, the wool caravan |
| **walk, laden** | **20** | average burden on a road. **The narrative's own figure — "one day's walk, call it fifteen to twenty miles laden" — sits just under this, which is the DMG being right about a party carrying rather more than 25 lb apiece** |
| **ride, medium mount** | **40** | "a long day's ride" to Draycot, whose walk is two days at 20 |
| **walk, off the road** | **10** | rugged. The half-hour up the outcrop above the basecamp |

*source: **DM CALL**, 2026-09-12, choosing a row from each DMG table above for
this campaign's ordinary conditions. The rates are the book's; the choice of
which row applies to the hundred's roads is mine and is recorded here so it is
the same number every time.*

> ### THE CORRECTION THIS TABLE FORCED
>
> **The overland map was built on a DM-invented 18 miles per cart-day**, reasoned
> off *"fifteen to twenty miles laden"* — which is a **WALKING** figure, and
> which was then applied to **carts**. The DMG gives a cart **25**.
>
> **Every travel TIME in the narrative is unchanged** — three days to Marchford is
> still three days, because that is what was played. **Only the mileage moves**,
> and it moves onto the book: Marchford **54 → 75 miles**, Ludbury **90 → 125**.
>
> *This is why the lookup came before the map. `CLAUDE.md`: no rules table is
> written twice, and a table that exists in the book should never be invented.*
