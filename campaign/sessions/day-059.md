## THE VIAL — RED ARSENIC, and the clue was in the record for twelve days

**Trebb's first words were the whole question:** *"Why's the glass smoked? That's not
decoration, that costs money. **Somebody paid extra so this could sit in the
dark.**"*

**REALGAR.** Dry, crystalline, coal-coloured. A **pigment**, not a mordant — Pell
had the vessel right and the trade wrong. **Poisonous, and it degrades in
sunlight**, which is the entire reason for the glass.

> **NOBODY IN THE PARTY WAS EVER EXPOSED, AND IT WAS EARNED.** Toller refused to
> uncork it under his roof. Pell said take it to a dyer's yard — open air, a water
> butt, a man who knows fumes. **Trebb opened it himself, in shade, with a wet rag
> over his mouth, with everyone else eight feet back where he put them.** Three good
> decisions in a row, and no saving throw was called for because none was warranted.

**Sold for 35 gp** to the church's limner *(valued 3d6=13 → 45 gp, a DM call
anchored on DMG p.28 pricing a limner at 10 s.p./day, the dearest hireling on the
table; he trimmed it because the party was passing through and said so)*.

## ⚠ THE HOLY WATER — THE PRIEST AND THE VESSEL ARE IN DIFFERENT TOWNS

**d6 = 3: Father Doule is FIFTH level** — the precise minimum DMG p.114 requires.
**d6 = 2: MARCHFORD HAS NO FONT** and never has had one. **That is exactly the thing
Cuddy said he could not vouch for.**

> *"I can make it. **I have never made it.** You want a basin of fine metal, blessed
> and engraved, sitting inside a proper font, and the whole made to order — and this
> is a bridge town with a wool trade and a leaking roof. **It has never once been the
> most urgent thing.**"*

**THE BOOK'S PRICE, already cached in `rules/equipment.md` from DMG pp.114-115:**
copper basin **130-180 gp** + font **200 gp** = **330-380 gp**, built to special
order in **2d4+2 = 4-10 weeks**, and thereafter **six vials a week, once a week,
for ever.** Blessed crystal vials 2-5 gp each. **One font per religious edifice,
ever.**

**Against 631 gp 6 sp in hand — and Marchford is three days from Wethersby on the
road the party travels constantly, with two carriers running it.**

> ### TWO THINGS TO STATE PLAINLY BEFORE ANYBODY SPENDS ANYTHING
>
> 1. **It would be the CHURCH'S font, not the party's** — the same shape as Fenn's
>    barn and Coll's basecamp. A permanent asset in somebody else's hands.
> 2. **What the party gets for it is whatever is agreed NOW**, before the money
>    moves. First call on the six vials a week is the obvious ask and it has not
>    been asked. **DO NOT let this be settled by assumption.**


### THE FONT — DECLINED, day 59. Recorded as a DECISION, not an omission

**The party considered it with the full book price in front of them and said no
for now.** 330-380 gp, 4-10 weeks, six vials a week for ever, and they had 631 gp
6 sp in hand.

**Nothing about the offer expires.** Father Doule is 5th level and will still be
5th level; DMG pp.114-115 will still price a copper font at the same figure; and
**one font per religious edifice means that wherever it eventually goes, it stays.**
**Cuddy's chapel is still eligible and Cuddy is still 3rd.**

> **DO NOT RE-RAISE IT UNPROMPTED.** It was weighed and declined. If holy water
> becomes urgent — and against undead it will — the party knows exactly what it
> costs and who can make it, which is more than they knew on day 45.
