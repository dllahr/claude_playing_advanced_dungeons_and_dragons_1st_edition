# DAYS 73-76 — THE DWARF TO DRAYCOT, AND A MAN HAS BEEN SENT

**One cart, one mule, two horses, eight of the nine** *(Hask is on the wool)*. Two
days down, one drover on the first morning at **32** who gave them the road and six
words, and three days of empty road either side.

## CUDDY IS FRAIL — d6 = 1, and it is not a crisis

**Twenty-two days on from ten nights at a chest by candle at his own expense.** The
wet is in his chest, he is seventy-odd, and it is November. **He is not dying. He is
old, and it shows, and it did not show in September.**

## AND TWO OF HIS OWN — one absent, one enormous

**d6 = 1: NOTHING BACK ABOUT WHAT A DWARF IS OWED.** *"Not a line. And I'll not put
him in the ground guessing."* **He goes into the chapel above ground on trestles, and
he waits, exactly as Cuddy said on day 51.**

> ### d6 = 6, then d6 = 5: SOMEBODY HAS BEEN SENT ABOUT THE ANIMATED DEAD
>
> **He has both letters on the table — the one he sent, as promised, and the one that
> came back.** His house has not written to ask questions. **It has written to say a
> man is coming.**
>
> *"I don't know when. I don't know what he'll be. **I know that in thirty years of
> writing to that house I have never once had a letter that said somebody was
> coming.**"*
>
> **He is not frightened. He is RESPONSIBLE, and it is the first time the party has
> seen that on him:** *"You told me a true thing and I did the correct thing with it
> and **now there's a man on a road because of it.** I'd do it again and I'd not enjoy
> it any more the second time."*
>
> **THE PARTY'S ANSWER, and it is the right one:** *"We're glad to talk to or work
> with whoever they send. Just let us know."* **No defensiveness, nothing hidden,
> and no attempt to get ahead of it.**

> ### ⚠ THE DOUBLE EDGE, for whoever runs the arrival
> **A man sent about undead is a man of that Church — and holy water requires a
> cleric of the FIFTH level** *(DMG p.114)*, **and this district has none and three
> 1st-level clerics.** *The thing coming toward the crag may be the answer to the
> crag.*
>
> **And on the other side:** the licence covers **stone and timber** and Wend has
> already found the hole about movables; the title is **unsettled**; and the party
> holds **Cuddy's fair copy of the grant to Rogahn and Zelligar, which the hall does
> not know exists.**

## THE EVENING — FOUR CLERICS, AND WHAT HE SAYS TO DELLIE

**Bread broken and the offices said by Yrtone, Blartho, Dellie and Cuddy** — the
first time there have been four in that chapel in years.

**And d6 = 4: he says one thing worth keeping, and it is the thing he noticed on day
51 and said nothing about.** *(`npcs.md` has carried it for twenty-three days:
"Cuddy notices her. He does not say anything about it. He notices.")*

> *"You said the offices right the last time you were here and you've said them right
> tonight, and **you've been at it five weeks and it doesn't sound like five weeks.**"*
>
> **"Now — I'd like you to know the words for him. Not today. In case."**
>
> *"I'm not being grand about it. **I'm seventy-odd and it's November and there's a
> man coming and I don't know when the answer comes back about what he's owed.** And
> somebody ought to be able to say it over him who knows what they're saying, **and
> there ought to be two of us who can and not one.**"*

**He teaches her the general words at the table and it takes about an hour. Blartho
and Yrtone sit in and do not interrupt.**

> **He asked for four things and said he would not ask for a fifth. This is not a
> fifth — it is housekeeping, and it is the most frightening sentence he has said to
> anybody.**

## ⚠ AND TWO THINGS THE PARTY KNOWS AND HAS NOT ACTED ON

**The chapel roof is bad at the north end**, patched with turf, and *"it's going to be
a wet winter."* **And an almoner's burial purse comes from the hall and is empty by
Candlemas most years** — he has buried people at his own cost and never mentions it.

**He has never asked for either, and will not.** He is now visibly frail in a building
with a hole in it. **`articles.md` is explicit about the only shape that works: a roof
mended by Coll's crew on a passing job, or a standing sum on Wend's books with HIS
name on it as the man who DISBURSES it. Not a purse across a table.**
