# DAY 76 EVENING / DAY 77 MORNING — THE ROOF QUESTION PUT TO THE REEVE, AND WEND HAS NO AMOUNTS

**Home off the Draycot road at dusk on the 76th. Two calls, eleven doors apart.**

## FIRST — A CORRECTION AGAINST THE DM, PLAYER-CAUGHT

**The DM told the player the Wace receipt was still travelling toward a Wend who had
never seen the letter.** *"Wend did see the receipt."* **She did — on day 65**, and
Toller paid his full ~250 gp against it the same day. **The ⚠ box in `articles.md`
art. 11 had been true on day 55 and had sat open for eleven days after it resolved.**
Closed in the file, with the general lesson written into it: **a ⚠ box is a promise
to come back and close it.** See art. 11.

## OTTIR — the chapel roof, and HE DOES NOT KNOW (d6 = 2)

**At his own door, shirtsleeves, bowl in hand, and he puts it down.** No reaction roll
— surety taken day 4, a horse found for him, backed in the hall, witnessed art. 13
four days ago, standing **+10%** loyalty adjustment.

He will not guess. **Draycot is not his hundred and an almonry's fabric is nothing he
has ever had cause to look at.** And then he asks the better question:

> ### **"WHOSE IS IT — THAT'S THE WRONG END OF IT. THEY ASK WHO MENDED IT LAST."**
>
> *"...and who paid the man. Do that twice and you've got your answer out of
> **custom**, which nobody can write a letter about. **And if nobody has, then it's
> nobody's, and that's precisely why it's got turf on it.**"*

**Three consequences, all of them cheap:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **THE MAN TO ASK IS CUDDY** | thirty years in that chapel. He will remember who came with the ladder. **One question on a trip already being made** |
| **PERRIN IS STRUCK OFF** | *"He can't spend a shilling and he refers upward. You found that out yourselves with your rosewood. Ask Perrin about a roof and Perrin asks Wace about a roof — same letter, somebody else's name on it, and you can't stop it"* |
| **AND HE REACHES CUDDY'S AND WEND'S WARNING FROM A THIRD DIRECTION** | *"A roof that's his is a roof he can charge somebody for. You'd not be learning the answer. **You'd be making it.**"* — Cuddy: *"you'd be arming him."* Wend: *"asking makes him look at it."* **No contact between the three of them** |

**HE WILL NOT CARRY IT** *(d6 = 4 — full counsel, no hand in it)*: *"I see the man
twice a year and I'd sooner both times were about sheep."* **Consistent, not cold.**

## WEND — the southern list, and SHE HAS NO AMOUNTS (d6 = 2)

**She does not reach for a paper, which is the tell.**

> *"You asked me for a list and I gave you a list. **A list isn't an order.** I've had
> a week and I spent it on the articles, and the buying never got costed. **That's
> mine, not yours.**"*

*(Same register as day 71 on the loom — states it, no excuse in front of it.)* **What
she wants before she will cost it: how much, and WHOSE money** — art. 13's rank is
wages first, and art. 7's 500 trades only on things **sellable before shearing**.
**Iron and salt are. A loom is not.**

### IT DOES NOT FIT ONE CART (d6 = 2) — and the arithmetic is one line

| | |
|---|---|
| One mule and one cart | **~800 lb** *(`rules/equipment.md`: 8,000 gp weight, standing DM call)* |
| Iron, nails and salt **last time** | **600 lb for 100 gp** — and she wants **more**, the roll having grown |

**The iron alone eats the cart**, before cloth to sell, shears, whatever Toller sends,
and a return load.

> ### ⚠ AND THE BINDING CONSTRAINT IS NOT WEIGHT — IT IS THAT THE SECOND CART IS AT MARROW'S
>
> **Halm and the other cart are with the wool party, back about the 83rd.** So: **two
> carts south means leaving after the 83rd**, or one cart now and buy about half.
>
> **HASK IS ON THAT SAME TRIP.** A southern trip that leaves now goes without the man
> who held his number through two silences at Ossery's on day 59.

### THE LOOM: BERN, AND SHE IS FLAT ABOUT IT (d6 = 1)

> *"Don't buy a loom four days away. **You'd be buying a thing nobody within a day of
> here can mend, and you'd have bought it off a man I'm already paying to build and
> mend looms.** I'd be handing Bern a rival and then paying him to keep it standing."*

**Bern is already halfway in** — the dismantled broadloom went through his hands and
he asked whether the frame is pegged or wedged (day 65).

**AND SHE NAMES HER OWN RISK UNPROMPTED:** Bern has never built one, so **he is slower
than a cart**, and Lyde has two trained weavers and one loom *now*.

> **HER FIX — buy the half of a loom that isn't joinery: the reed, the heddles, a
> shuttle or two.** *"A joiner can make you a frame. He cannot make you a reed."*
> Light, cheap, fits in anything.
>
> **AND SHE WILL NOT WRITE THAT ORDER:** *"Ask Bern what he wants off a Ludbury list.
> Don't guess for him and don't order it over his head — he's the one building it."*
> **Consistent with day 65: "it's your business and I'll not touch it."**

### ONE THING ADDED SINCE DAY 71: FLAX SEED (d6 = 3)

**Dill's proposal came to her and she has been chewing it.** She still says *don't cart
flax north* — **but seed is not a crop.**

> *"Seed's a sack. **It's the one flax thing in the world worth four days on a cart,
> because the rest of it you grow here or you don't have it at all.**"*

**No price, and she does not know the sowing month.** And **she has not asked the
eleven about Gyll's field yet** — Ottir's condition stands: **one at a time, and
nobody goes near Gyll first.**
