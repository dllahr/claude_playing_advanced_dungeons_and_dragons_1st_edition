# Session record — campaign day 55

*Preceded by `day-054.md`; followed by `day-059.md`. Full list: `campaign/sessions/`.*

# DAY 55 — TOLLER. 76, AND HE FINDS THE SAME HOLE WEND FOUND

**He is glad to see them and glad of the news for its own sake**, and he reads the
licence twice, slowly, the way he reads everything.

> *"Wace's own hand. **And he's dated it back.** — He'll not have done that to be
> kind, he'll have done it to make his page tidy, which is better than kind because
> it'll still be tidy in ten years."*

### AND THEN — d6 = 1

> *"**Stone.** And timber."*
>
> *"That's what it says. That's the whole of what it says. **It is not a bill of
> sale for a dead wizard's balances**, and if the day ever comes that somebody asks
> me where I got two hundredweight of brass instruments, I cannot hold this up. I
> can hold up a licence to break rock."*

> **⚠ TWO INDEPENDENT READERS, FOUR HOURS APART, FOUND THE SAME DEFECT.** Wend on
> the night of the 54th and Toller on the morning of the 55th — **the two most
> commercially careful people in this district, neither having spoken to the
> other.** That is corroboration, not coincidence, and it settles the question of
> whether the hole is real. **It is real.**

### WHAT WOULD FIX IT — and it is the thing he named on day 42

**Coin, or Master Wace's own hand.** He is not moving the goalposts; that has been
his condition since the 42nd and it is unchanged. What he needs is **not a new
licence** — just **a line from Wace acknowledging that the movables came off the
hill with his knowledge.**

> *"He was told. You told me you told him, out loud, and that he wrote it down. **So
> ask him for the sentence.** Not the licence — I'm not asking him to grant you
> anything. **Ask him to confirm what he already wrote in his own book.**"*

> ### ⚠ AND THIS PUTS TWO GOOD ADVISERS IN DIRECT OPPOSITION — the party's call
>
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **WACE, day 52** | *"The renewal is my business and not the hall's. **Write to me.**"* — **a named correspondent, invited** |
> | **WEND, day 54** | tell him what you took **in the Michaelmas letter, with the money** — and **ask him for NOTHING**, because *"asking makes him look at it"* |
> | **TOLLER, day 55** | **ask him now.** Michaelmas is a year off and the glass is sitting in a locked room he does not want it in |
>
> **Both are right and they point opposite ways.** Wend is right that a request
> makes a steward re-read the instrument. Toller is right that ~250 gp and a
> dealer's exposure should not wait a year. **Nobody manufactured this — it falls
> straight out of three separate things three people said.**

### WHAT HE OFFERS TODAY — d6 = 2, and he tells them it is bad

**90 gp, cash, this morning, no questions** — against his own valuation of ~250.

> *"That's a receiver's price and I'll not dress it up. **It's what the risk costs
> me**, and I'd think less of you for taking it, and I'd still hand you the coin."*

**Otherwise it stays exactly where it is: his room, his judgement, open terms,
unsold and unpaid — the same footing as the diary, the plaque and the headboard.**

### THE 86 MARKS — d6 = 2. No progress, and no excuses

He has been at it and **the papers will not give it up.** The most recent date on
any of them is thirty-plus years old, and the tick marks do not tie to anything he
can find.

> *"I've had four goes at it and I've got nothing, and I'd rather tell you that than
> keep it warm."*

### AND SARN CAME — d6 = 5, volunteered unasked

**It went well and Toller brings it up himself**, which is the tell.

> *"Your lad came. **On his own two legs and on his own account**, and he'd got
> three questions written down on a bit of slate, and one of them was a good one."*
>
> *"He can't read yet and he knows what a ledger is, which is the harder half and
> nobody can teach it. **Send him again.**"*

*(He does not say which question was the good one and nobody presses him.)*
