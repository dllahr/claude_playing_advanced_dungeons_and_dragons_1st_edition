# The party's journal — what they have FOUND and what they make of it

**Player-facing. Nothing in this file is DM-side secret.** It records things the
characters have actually seen, in their own reckoning, and the questions they have
asked out loud. Distinct from the **map database** (geography — `cells.seen` is
what they have looked at) and the campaign TSVs (numbers): this is the *record of
discoveries*, and the only place the party's own **conclusions** live.

## THE EIGHTY-SIX MARKS — area 2, the kitchen. Expedition 6, day 37, round 177

Found by **Tristle**, on the **underside of the long table's edge**, third bay down
the north wall — lying on his back with a light, during the fourteen-turn search.
Counted twice by him and again by Frython. They agree.

> **Eighty-six marks**, cut into the wood with a knife point. Groups of five, four
> upright and one struck through.
>
> **Neat at the start**, in even rows. Less neat later. **Ragged at the end.**
>
> **No name. No date. No word.** Only the count — and a **last mark deeper and
> longer than the rest**, as though the hand pressed and stayed.

**Eighty-six days. Somebody sat in this kitchen and counted them, and then
stopped.**

**Yrtone, aloud, after fourteen turns of saying nothing:** *"They didn't all leave."*

### What the party knows that bears on it

- Zelligar and Rogahn marched out on a campaign and **never came back**. The
  stronghold has stood since.
- **Frython read the dates in Zelligar's diary** in Wethersby and cracked the
  names out of it. The diary is with **Toller** now, unsold.
- The most recent date on any paper recovered from area 6 is **more than thirty
  years old**.

**Nobody has counted eighty-six days back from anything yet.** That arithmetic has
not been done, and there is a diary five days south that might carry a date to
count from.

## THE ROOM NOTHING ENTERS — area 2

**A fact the party found and has not explained.**

Something in Quasqueton is **collecting metal**. It has prised the ironwork out of
the trophy-room wall, taken **eleven of the party's spears**, and dropped one of
the trophy-room hanging irons on the road at the (16,19) crossing, where it lay
trodden into the dirt for days.

**Forty feet from that crossing is a kitchen with several hundred pounds of iron
in it** — pots, pans, racks, hooks, and a cast-iron kettle on a chain.

**Its east door was shut and UNLOCKED. Nothing has opened it in months.** The dust
inside the room is unbroken wall to wall. Ista read the jamb and the floor sweep
and was certain.

**They walk past it twice a day and they do not go in.** No one has an explanation.

## The road, and who walks it

- The **outer door of Quasqueton stood open** when the party arrived, sixteen days
  after they closed it behind them.
- The dust at the threshold is **churned** — many crossings, over days, both
  directions, mostly **small and barefoot**, with **something considerably bigger
  in a hard-soled boot** among them in two or three places.
- The traffic goes **north up the x=16 corridor — under the magic mouths** — and
  at the crossing it turns **north**, not east or west.
- **Seventy feet of the y=19 corridor and the whole of area 2 are clean.** Their
  road is one line and the party has come in behind it.
