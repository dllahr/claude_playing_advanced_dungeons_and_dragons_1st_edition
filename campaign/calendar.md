**COLL'S BASECAMP** was built by his crew while the party was at Ludbury and is
**thirty minutes from the cave mouth and thirty minutes from the road.** Graded
cart-passable trail, a cleared and levelled flat, a fire pit, a latrine well
downwind, **a boxed spring that runs clear**, and a turf-and-pole lean-to that
sleeps eight dry with firewood stacked under it. A board on the post reads
**KEEP DRY. FILL WOOD.** Nobody asked them to build the spring or the lean-to.

**The patrol never went past the crag turning**, which is the outer limit of the
day's-walk circuit and the point where the party's own road ends and the hills
begin.
