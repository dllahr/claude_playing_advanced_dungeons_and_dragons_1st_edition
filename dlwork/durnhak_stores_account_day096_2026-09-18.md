## THE DISCREPANCY THAT MATTERS, AND IT IS NOT THE CART

On the 95th Tristle told Durnhak the stores would come to **about 18 gp**
of his seventy-five. That figure carried **nails at ~2 gp for 20 lb**,
which was an unsourced guess made at the table and is **too low**: a
wrought nail is bar iron plus a smith's time, and bar alone lands in this
district at 0.267 gp/lb. Rolled honestly on declared bands (d6=5):
**0.5 gp/lb, so 20 lb of big timber nails is 10 gp, not 2.**

## THE ROPE QUESTION, RULED

Genuinely ambiguous, and the convenient answer was not allowed to win.
Tristle asked *"do you want some now or wait and get us when we meet
you"*, which reads as **one order partly advanced**; against that, the six
coils he took are being consumed slinging 588 lb up a watercourse for four
days and he asked for *"good rope, not the cheap sort."* **Rolled d6=3:
the six already given DISCHARGE the six on the list. Nothing more to buy.**

The same logic then applies to the oil: **3 of the 12 flasks are already
delivered, so nine remain.**

Six fresh coils would cost **2 gp 4 sp** and weigh 45 lb. If the party
wants to bring them anyway it is the cheapest goodwill on this list.
