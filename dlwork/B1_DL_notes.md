# 2026-08-17
Almost had a TPK - attacked by a pair of troglodytes that went down quickly, and then by a second pair that ripped through the party taking down everyone except my best fighter (lots of bad rolls by us, good rolls by them), who managed to drag them to a nearby room (already explored) and bar the doors (had to fight off some giant rats to do that).  Then it was a matter of getting water for the party to drink while they recovered - he managed to do that for a couple days fighting off rats, kobolds, giant centipede.  But then another pack of giant rats took him down - that was my mistake - I should have just stepped into the room and held the door against them, I thought it would be an easy fight, but then they rolled 2 20's and an 18.  The DM went lenient on me and ruled that he had killed enough of them that they didn't run rampant, the weakened characters in the room got up and yelled and managed to scare off the remaining rats, and dragged the fighter into the room and rebared the door.  Elf recovered enough to sneak out to get water, and 7 days later the party is back in action - badly scarred, not maimed.
